On the wearer's profile, only if they turn it on: 🔒 Locked · 7 days

Decisions made overnight, for the owner to confirm

  1. The wearer starts a lock (they're the one putting the device on): on their own (self-lock) or with a keyholder they're in an active directed dynamic with. One lock at a time.
  2. Options from the tracker as presets (A full lockdown, B one release a week with permission, C routine without release) plus "Custom". Each has a rule and a release policy: none / keyholder decides / routine.
  3. The keyholder can add or remove time, hide or show the time left, change the rules, turn daily verification on or off, set how long a hygiene opening may last, allow a release, and unlock.
  4. Safety: the wearer can always end the lock with "Emergency unlock"; it's recorded and the keyholder is told. Nothing here can stop someone taking a device off.
  5. Verification: a code for the day (different every day, per lock) to write on paper in the photo, like Chaster. Photos are re-encoded (no location data) and seen only by the wearer and their keyholder.
  6. When the dynamic ends the keyholder loses access at once; the lock carries on as a self-lock the wearer controls.
  7. Profile: a "Locked · N days" badge only if the wearer turns it on (off by default).
  8. Not yet: Chaster's games (wheel of fortune, dice, card games), public "pillory" voting, reward store, and links into homework tasks.

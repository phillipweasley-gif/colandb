Decisions made overnight, for the owner to confirm

  1. Starter types (each a pair of sides; the site team can't edit them yet): Dominant/submissive · Daddy/boy · Daddy/girl · Mommy/boy · Owner/property · Master/slave · Keyholder/chastity wearer · Handler/pup · Trainer/trainee · Mentor/mentee · Top/bottom · Caregiver/little; and equal ones: Partners · Married · Play partners · Leather family · Friends.
  2. The leading side (Dominant, Daddy, Mommy, Owner, Master, Keyholder, Handler, Trainer, Mentor, Top, Caregiver) is the one who will be able to assign homework and manage chastity. Equal types give nobody those powers.
  3. Consent: nothing happens until the other member accepts. Either member can end an active dynamic at any time, with no approval needed; ending takes effect immediately.
  4. On profiles: an active dynamic shows on both profiles only if both leave "Show on my profile" on (default on). Pending and ended ones are never shown.
  5. Connections: members in an active dynamic count as connections, so "My connections" visibility finally means something.
  6. Limits: at most 10 pending invitations sent at once; one active or pending dynamic of the same type between the same two members.

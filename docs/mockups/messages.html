3 · A message request, and the report sheet.

Decisions for you

  1. Who can message: any full member can message any other. If you're not connected (no active dynamic), it arrives under Requests; nothing goes to your Inbox until you accept. You can turn off requests entirely.
  2. Block: they can't message you, see your profile or your feed posts, propose a dynamic, or find you; they aren't told. Your past conversation disappears for both of you. Unblock any time from Account → Blocked members.
  3. Report: pick a reason; the site team gets an email and can read only the reported conversation's last 20 messages (otherwise admins can't read messages). Optional "Also block them".
  4. Text only to start (up to 2,000 characters). Photos in messages could come later.
  5. Notifications: unread count on the Messages tab and in the account menu; an in-site notification for new requests. A new page load shows new messages (no live chat yet).
  6. Delete: you can delete a conversation from your side; the other person keeps their copy.
  7. Limits: at most 20 new requests a day per member, to stop spam.

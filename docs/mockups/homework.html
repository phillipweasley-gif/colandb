Tasks & rules

Add or change tasks, the proof each needs, your standard phrase, and the consequence ladder.

Decisions made overnight, for the owner to confirm

  1. Who: only the leading side of an active directed dynamic can create a program for the other member. If the dynamic ends, the program is archived at once: the lead loses access to it (including proof photos); the member keeps their own history.
  2. Weeks run Monday to Sunday, like the tracker. Each task has a weekly minimum; "Met / Needs work" and "Quotas met X/N" are calculated.
  3. Day statuses: Done, Missed, Moved (rescheduled and communicated), N/A. The member logs Done / Moved / N/A; only the lead marks Missed.
  4. Proof: none, text, photo, photo + text, or written report. Photos are re-encoded (no camera or location data), stored privately, and visible only to the member and their lead.
  5. Review: the lead can accept a logged day or mark it "not good enough" with a note; the member is notified either way.
  6. Consequence ladder: free-text levels written by the lead (level, examples, correction), shown to the member. Logging a consequence as its own task comes later.
  7. Not yet: due-time reminders, chastity choice (release 0.8.0), and a month calendar of completions (included as a simple month view).

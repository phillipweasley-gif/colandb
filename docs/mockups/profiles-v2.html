Step 6 · Health (optional, starts hidden)

Decisions made overnight, for the owner to confirm

  1. Steps: 1 Photo & bio · 2 Basics (location, hosting, active level) · 3 Identity & roles · 4 Stats (height, weight, body type) · 5 Kinks & limits · 6 Health (optional). Each saves on its own; skip anytime.
  2. New fields: Gender, Position, Expression, Height, Weight, Body type, How active, Not looking for, Kinks (rated, with giving/receiving), Hard limits, Hosting, Safer-sex practices, Last tested, Substances. Bio grows to 1,000 characters.
  3. Left out on purpose: HIV status as its own field (practices + last tested cover it with less exposure) and endowment. Both are easy to add if wanted.
  4. Health starts hidden even when filled in: the one exception to "filled = shown".
  5. Starter lists are trimmed from FetLife/Sniffies (e.g. ~35 roles instead of 813). The site team edits them in wp-admin; nothing is lost if renamed.
  6. Desktop tabs Activity, Dynamics, Calendar and Pictures appear as their releases land.

## The short version

- **You're in control of what you share.** Every profile detail has its own Show switch. Health details start hidden. Hidden means hidden: other members never see it, and member search never finds it.
- **Members only.** Profiles, photos, posts, messages and member search are only for signed-in, verified members who are 18 or older. Search engines and the public never see them.
- **We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers.**
- **Your messages are private.** Our tools don't let administrators read conversations, unless one of you reports it. Then the site team can read that one conversation to look into the report.
- **Photos lose their location data.** We re-save every photo you upload, which strips camera and GPS information.
- **Leaving is easy.** You can download a copy of your data or ask us to delete it from your Account tab.
- **No AI and no automated decisions.** People, not algorithms, review reports, and we never feed your content to AI tools.

The rest of this statement explains the details.

## 1. Who we are

Central Ohio Leather & Beyond ("COL&B", "we", "us") is a volunteer community project, not an incorporated company or registered nonprofit. It runs colandb.com, a community site for Central Ohio's queer leather, kink and BDSM communities, with a public events calendar and a private member area. The project's organizer is responsible for how your information is handled.

**Contact:** foxxyforce@gmail.com. Email is the quickest way to reach us. If you need a postal address for a legal request, email us and we'll provide one.

## 2. Who this applies to

This statement covers colandb.com, including the public calendar and the member area. It covers anyone who visits the site, subscribes to event updates, RSVPs, submits an event, or creates an account.

**You must be 18 or older to create an account.** We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and turn away anyone under 18. We don't ask for ID documents, so we keep no copies of them. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we close it and delete their information. Adults-only means adults-only; please tell us at foxxyforce@gmail.com if you see someone who shouldn't be here.

## 4. Sensitive information

Much of what members share here is sensitive: sexual orientation, sex life and kinks, health and HIV-prevention details, photos, and relationships. We treat all of it that way:

- **It's your choice.** Nothing beyond your display name, date of birth and email is required.
- **Health details start hidden.**
- **It's never public.** Member content is only shown to signed-in, verified adult members. Member pages tell search engines not to index them, and private photos are served only to people allowed to see them.
- **Only shown answers are searchable.** Member search only matches what you show to all members, so a hidden or connections-only answer can't be found by searching.
- **We never sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.**

**Blocking.** If you block someone, neither of you can see the other's profile, posts or conversation, follows and nods between you are removed, and any dynamic between you ends. They aren't told.

**Site administrators.** Our member area's tools don't let administrators read hidden profile answers or private messages. A small number of volunteer administrators, and our hosting provider, have technical access to the server and database. They use it only to keep the site running, to look into a report, or when the law requires it.

## 6. How we use your information

- To run the calendar and the member area, and to show members what you've chosen to share.
- To send the emails and notifications you ask for, plus account emails such as verification, password resets and privacy requests.
- To keep the community safe: age checks, reviewing reports, enforcing blocks, and limits that stop spam (for example on new message requests and nods).
- To keep an **audit log** of important account events (for example sign-up, changes to who can see your profile, reports, privacy requests). The log records *that* something changed, not your personal details, so it can't become a second copy of your profile.
- To meet legal obligations.

We don't use your information for advertising, profiling for marketing, or automated decisions about you. **We don't use AI or machine learning on your profile, photos, posts or messages**, and we don't give your content to anyone to train AI. Reports and moderation decisions are made by people. If you think a decision about your account was wrong, email foxxyforce@gmail.com and someone other than the person who made it will review it where we can.

## 8. Safety and moderation

- **Reports.** You can report a post, a conversation or a member. When you report a conversation, the site team can read **that whole conversation** to look into it. They can't read any others. Opening a report is recorded in the audit log. The report email we receive contains a link, not the messages.
- **Blocks** work as described in section 5.
- **Message requests.** Members you're not connected with can send you at most three messages until you accept. You can turn requests off. Requests are limited per day to stop spam.

## 9. Photos and location

- Every photo you upload, including profile, cover, feed, homework proof and chastity verification photos, is **re-saved by our server**, which removes camera details and GPS location.
- Profile photos follow your Show switch. Feed photos follow the post's audience. Homework and verification photos are visible only to the people in that dynamic.
- Your location is whatever city, region and country you type in. We don't collect precise location from your device.

## 11. Your choices and rights

- **Download a copy of your data.** Request it from the Account tab, confirm by email, and we'll send you a copy.

- **Hide yourself from member search,** turn off message requests, choose who sees each post, and stop sharing the events you're going to.
- **Turn off any kind of notification,** and unsubscribe from event emails.
- **Block and report** other members.

You can also email foxxyforce@gmail.com about any of these, or to correct your date of birth. We'll answer within 30 days.

## 12. Security

We use HTTPS throughout. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Every form is protected against forged requests. Private photos are served only after checking who's asking, and are never cached publicly. Member pages are marked "do not index". No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

# Changelog: COL&B Silent Auction

## 0.1.0

**First release: silent auctions on WooCommerce** (owner, 2026-11-11: "I need to build out a fundraising section that allows for a silent auction using the wordpress/WooCommerce integration". Chose our own plugin with WooCommerce for checkout; bidders see the top bid; anyone with a registered account may bid; payments through the Weasley Studios Square account, which must capture sales tax and processing fees).

- Auctions and items in wp-admin (Silent Auction menu), a never-cached Auction page (`[csa_auction]`, created as a draft), bidding for signed-in accounts with minimum raises, Buy It Now, last-minute extension and safe handling of simultaneous bids.
- Bidder names are never public. The top bid and bid count are.
- At close, one WooCommerce order per winner: winning bids plus sales tax, no other fees. Paid orders add the winning bids, less the auction's card-costs percentage, to the linked fundraiser (needs Community Events Calendar 1.52.0).
- **Compliance changes after research** (owner, 2026-11-11: "make sure we're following all local, state, and federal rules"; report in the project files, "Silent auction compliance in Ohio"):
  - No card fee is added on top. A required surcharge breaks Visa/Mastercard rules (none on debit cards, 3% cap) and Square supports none online. The owner chose to have bids include card costs: the page says "Bids include card processing costs (about 3%), which are paid from the winning bid. The rest goes to: …", and that percentage is taken off before the fundraiser total.
  - Bids, starting bids, minimum raises and Buy It Now are whole dollars (owner: "The bid should be in round numbers").
  - Per auction: who collects the payments (shown: "Payments are collected for COL&B by …"), days to pay (default 7; the winner email gives the date), a not-tax-deductible notice (default: "COL&B is not a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit yet, so winning bids and donated items are not tax-deductible.") shown on the page and in the winner email, and written auction terms (default 8 points) shown on the page, linked from each bid form and the winner email.
  - "Value" is now "Est. value $… (from the donor)".
  - The research also found an open question the plugin can't settle: Ohio's auctioneer licensing law (R.C. 4707) covers online silent auctions and its charity exemption needs a tax-exempt sponsor. The owner has been asked to check with the Ohio Department of Agriculture before launch.
- **Fixed-price sale format** (owner, 2026-11-11: "let's do it as a standard sale for now with the ability to update it as an auction moving forward"). Research found Ohio's auctioneer licensing law (R.C. 4707) covers online silent auctions and its charity exemption only covers 501(c)(3)s, and COL&B is forming as a 501(c)(4). Each auction now has a Format: *fixed-price sale* (the default for new ones) or *silent auction*. In a sale each item has one price (its Buy It Now price), the first signed-in person to buy it gets it, their order is created at once and they go straight to the pay page, with a purchase email holding the pay link. Two people buying at the same moment can't both get it. Cancelling an unpaid order in a sale that is still open puts the item back on sale. Paid sales count toward the fundraiser like auction wins (less the card-costs percentage). The page, terms, messages and the admin summary use sale wording. Switching an auction to sale (or back) switches the standard terms too.
- Default tax notice is now "COL&B is not a 501(c)(3) charity, so payments and donated items are not tax-deductible." (COL&B is forming as a 501(c)(4), so this stays true.)
- Five plain-text emails: purchase (sale), outbid, bought with Buy It Now, winner, and a summary to the site admin. **The owner must approve their exact wording before this is promoted to live** (`/mnt/project-files/silent-auction/auction-emails.txt` in the project, and the pull request).
- WooCommerce's Order attribution tracking is kept off (no analytics on the site).

**Verified** in the local WordPress kit with WooCommerce 11.2.2 on SQLite: new `tests/auction-e2e.sh` 82/82. The sale part covers saving the format (terms switch), the sale page (prices, no bidding), buying straight to the pay page, the order (price + 8% tax, no fee), the purchase email, a second buyer refused, two buyers at the same moment (exactly one order), cancel puts it back on sale (but not once paid), the fundraiser getting the price less 3%, a closed sale refusing purchases, and the end-of-sale summary. The auction part covers wp-admin entry over real HTTP, the page (prices, pickup, payment terms, who collects, not-tax-deductible notice, auction terms, no-store header, no tracking scripts), the bid rules and messages, the outbid email, signed-out and expired-form bids, two equal bids at the same moment (exactly one wins), Buy It Now, not-yet-open auctions, draft items, the last-minute extension, whole-dollar bids, closing once (orders with 8% tax and no fee, emails), the pay page adding no fee, the winner's pay page (and nobody else's), paying (fundraiser total up once, by the winning bid less 3%), member access to wp-admin and no PHP warnings. Also `tests/fundraisers-e2e.sh` 21/21 and `tests/form-cache-e2e.sh` 36/36 with this plugin and WooCommerce active. Screenshots of the page (desktop and phone) were taken in the kit.

**Not tested:** a real Square payment (the kit has no Square account; the paid step is simulated by setting the order to Processing, which is what a successful Square payment does), and how the page looks inside the live site's Elementor theme.
